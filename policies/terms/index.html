Terms

PainMap content is provided for public research, education, and advocacy analysis. Numbers should be read with their stated assumptions, source type, and uncertainty.

  • This site is not medical, veterinary, legal, investment, or policy advice.
  • Event estimates and country proxy context are different kinds of evidence.
  • External source links are provided for auditability and may change outside PainMap's control.
  • Reuse sensitive claims only after checking the linked source and current caveats.
