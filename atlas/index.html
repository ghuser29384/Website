Layers

What the atlas can compare

proxy

Human burden indicators

World Bank country indicators for poverty, mortality, pollution, WASH, conflict, and related burden proxies.

Vintage: runtime World Bank 2010:2025 window. Uncertainty: low confidence.

proxy

Factory-farmed animals

Country and world counts from OWID/Fishcount-style public data, weighted only with visible welfare assumptions.

Vintage: latest available runtime rows plus 2026-05-31.atlas.2 release. Uncertainty: low confidence.

proxy

Wild animals and insects

Land-area and insecticide proxies used as directional context, not as settled welfare measurements.

Vintage: 2026-05-31.atlas.2 release. Uncertainty: very low confidence.

priority overlay

Animal priority overlay

A decision-support layer combining proxy scale, event-evidence salience, and tractability notes. It is not a direct pain measurement.

Vintage: 2026-05-31.atlas.2 release. Uncertainty: very low confidence.

boundary

Country and ADM1 boundaries

Natural Earth Admin 0 country geometry and geoBoundaries ADM1 geometry loaded only when a place is selected.

Vintage: Natural Earth 1:50m plus current geoBoundaries API. Uncertainty: low confidence.
