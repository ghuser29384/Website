About

A static research visualization

PainMap makes animal pain research easier to inspect by pairing event-level estimates with transparent caveats and place-first atlas context. It is not a clinical, veterinary, or personal health product.

Purpose

Make evidence legible

The site exists to help readers understand animal pain estimates without hiding uncertainty.

Governance

Correction-first posture

Source corrections, accessibility reports, and governance questions should be filed through the project issue tracker.

Boundary

No personal data workflow

Any future symptom, account, upload, or clinician feature should live behind a separate compliant service boundary.
