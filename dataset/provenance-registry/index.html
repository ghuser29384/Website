Direct

Source-level welfare estimates tied to a defined event, species, system, and time window.

Modeled

Values combining empirical observations with assumptions about duration, intensity, or production context.

Proxy

Directional place-level scale indicators built from public datasets and stated welfare assumptions.

Priority overlay

Decision-support overlays that combine proxies, evidence salience, and tractability notes without claiming direct pain totals.

boundary

Boundary

Geometry layers used to locate countries or ADM1 regions; not pain estimates.
