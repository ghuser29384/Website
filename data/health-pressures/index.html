Coverage

Disease, violence, nutrition, maternal health, and environmental exposure

These indicators are contextual evidence about conditions that may generate or accompany pain. They are not measurements of pain intensity, duration, prevalence, disability, or total suffering.

Comparison rules

Rank only within the same indicator

Each observation includes a rank and adverse percentile calculated only among countries with the same indicator. Cross-indicator addition, averaging, or universal ranking is prohibited because percentage prevalence, incidence rates, mortality rates, and pollutant concentrations are not commensurable.

Provenance

Latest non-null World Bank API observation

For every country and indicator, PainMap selects the latest numeric observation from 2010 through 2026, records the observation year, source organization, API URL, retrieval timestamp, response metadata, payload checksum, transform version, unit, caveat, and coverage count.
